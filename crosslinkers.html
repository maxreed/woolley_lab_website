Cross-linkers

Thiol-reactive, photoswitchable azobenzene cross-linkers developed in the lab — spanning UV to red-light switching. To request material, please contact us.

Contact the lab →

IAC — iodoacetamide azobenzene

Thiol-reactive, reversible photoswitchable cross-linker. The trans form is stable in the dark and absorbs UV (λmax 370 nm; ε370 = 24,000 M⁻¹cm⁻¹).

Reference ↗

BSBCA — bis-sulfonato-bis-chloroacetamido-azobenzene

Water-soluble, thiol-reactive cross-linker switched with UV light (ε370 = 29,000 M⁻¹cm⁻¹).

Reference ↗

TOM — tetra-ortho-methoxyazobenzene-chloroacetamide

Green/red photoswitchable cross-linker (λmax = 490 nm).

Reference ↗

PIP — tetra-ortho-methoxy azonium

Red-light photoswitch (ε600 ≈ 20,000 M⁻¹cm⁻¹ in pH 7.0 buffer); the trans azonium form has an apparent pKa of 7.2.
